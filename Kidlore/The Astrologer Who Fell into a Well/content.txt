The Star Gazer’s Fall

Zeno was a very clever man who loved to study the night sky. He spent every single day reading big books about the bright stars and the distant, swirling planets far above him in the universe.

Zeno believed the stars could tell him secrets about the future. He wore a long, purple robe covered in silver moons. He carried a heavy, golden telescope everywhere he went to see every tiny light clearly.

One evening, the sun went down and the sky turned a deep, dark blue. Zeno was very excited. He stepped out of his small stone house to begin his nightly walk and study the glowing moon.

The air was cool and the grass was soft under his feet. Zeno held his telescope up to his eye. He did not notice the rocks or the bushes in his path on this very evening.

Zeno gazed at a shimmering constellation that looked like a giant lion. He was so busy counting the stars in its mane that he forgot to look at where he was actually walking on the ground.

Near the edge of the field sat an old, deep well made of grey stones. It was very dark and very quiet. Zeno marched right toward the hole without even a single, careful glance at it.

Suddenly, Zeno’s foot slipped on a loose rock near the edge. He waved his arms wildly in the air, trying to grab something. With a loud splash, he fell into the cold, wet water far below.

The water was freezing and the walls of the well were very slippery. Zeno tried to climb out, but he kept sliding back down. He was wet, tired, and very, truly scared of the dark pit.

Zeno began to shout as loudly as he possibly could. "Help me! Someone please help me!" His voice echoed up the stone walls of the deep well and across the silent, grassy field in the night.

A kind neighbor named Lyra was walking home from the village nearby. She heard a strange, muffled voice coming from the ground. She stopped and listened carefully to the dark, chilly, and very quiet night air.

Lyra followed the sound until she reached the old stone well. She peered over the edge into the darkness. She saw poor Zeno splashing around in the water far down at the very bottom of it.

"Zeno! Is that you?" Lyra asked with great surprise. She quickly grabbed a nearby rope to help him. "What are you doing down there in the middle of this very dark, cold and lonely winter night?"

Zeno shivered and replied, "I was looking at the amazing stars to see what will happen tomorrow. I did not see the well at all." He looked up with deep and total and very clear embarrassment.

Lyra pulled him out and sighed. "You try to see what is in the heavens," she said kindly, "but you do not see what is right here on the earth at your very own walking feet."

The moral of the story is that we should always pay attention to the world around us. While it is good to dream, we must also watch where we are going in our everyday real lives.
