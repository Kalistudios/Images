The Clever Dance of Caper

Once there was a little goat named Caper. He loved to jump over rocks and eat the sweet, green grass in the field.

Caper did not see the big, gray wolf hiding. The wolf's name was Vulf. He was hungry and watched the small goat play.

Vulf jumped out from a dark bush. Caper was very scared. He knew he could not run away from the fast, mean wolf.

"Please do not eat me yet," Caper said softly. "I have a big wish. I want to dance one more time for you today."

Vulf looked at the small kid. He liked music and he liked to watch dancing. He thought he had plenty of time for lunch.

"You have a flute," Caper said to the wolf. "If you play a loud song, I will dance my very best dance for you now."

Vulf pulled out a shiny flute. He wanted to show Caper how well he could play. He took a very deep, big breath.

Vulf began to play a very loud song. The music went up into the sky. He closed his eyes to play even better.

Caper danced around in a circle. He kicked his tiny feet high. He moved closer and closer to the tall, thick green bushes.

The music was so loud that Vulf did not hear anything else. He was very proud of his silly, noisy flute song today.

Suddenly, Caper saw his chance to escape. He jumped over a rock and ran toward the safety of the big, safe farm nearby.

Vulf finished his song and opened his eyes. He looked around for the dancing goat, but the field was empty and quiet now.

Vulf saw Caper far away. The wolf was too late. He had been tricked by a small goat who was very, very clever.

Vulf felt very silly and put his flute away. He learned that he should not play when he had important work to do.

Moral: Do not let your pride distract you. Use your wits to stay safe. A clever mind is better than having big teeth.
