The Sweet, Sticky Trap

A big glass jar of golden honey sat on the table. Suddenly, it tipped over and a sweet puddle began to spread.

Theron the fly smelled the sugary scent from across the room. He called his friend Sips to come see the golden prize.

The two flies zoomed down to the table. They landed right in the middle of the thick, warm, and very sticky honey.

"This is the best treat I have ever tasted," Theron buzzed happily. He rubbed his tiny front legs together with great joy.

Sips agreed and began to drink the sweet syrup. She did not want to leave this wonderful mountain of delicious golden food.

They ate and ate until their bellies were full. They should have flown away then, but they wanted to eat even more.

Theron waded deeper into the golden pool. The honey felt warm on his feet, so he decided to take a big swim.

Sips followed him into the deep part. She did not notice that the honey was starting to hold onto her thin legs.

"It is time to go now," Theron said. He tried to lift his wings, but they were coated in heavy, thick syrup.

Sips tried to jump into the air. Her feet were stuck tight to the table. The honey would not let her go.

They pulled and they tugged with all their strength. But the more they moved, the more the sticky honey trapped their bodies.

"We were too greedy," Theron cried sadly. He could no longer move his legs or his wings. He was stuck very fast.

Sips looked at the beautiful honey. It was no longer a treat. Now, it was a trap that held them both down.

The sun went down and the flies remained still. They had traded their freedom for just a few moments of sweet honey.

The moral is: do not let greed take control of you. Too much of a good thing can quickly become a very dangerous trap.
