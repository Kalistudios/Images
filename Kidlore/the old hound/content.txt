The Old Hound

Bran was a very old dog. He lived in a warm cottage with his owner, Cedric. Bran had grey fur around his nose, and his legs moved much slower than before now.

Years ago, Bran was the fastest hound in the land. He and Cedric spent every single day running through the deep green woods. They always brought home the very best forest prizes.

Cedric would cheer as Bran chased after the quick stags. Bran was strong and his teeth were very sharp. No animal could ever escape his grip when he was young and fast.

But time changed everything for the old hound. Bran’s eyes were cloudy, and his joints felt stiff in the mornings. He preferred to nap by the fire instead of running outside today.

One sunny morning, Cedric grabbed his heavy boots. "Come on, Bran!" he shouted. They went into the forest to find a big wild boar. Bran tried his very best to keep up.

Suddenly, a huge boar jumped out from the bushes. Bran barked and lunged forward with all his might. He caught the boar by the ear, just like he did in the past.

Bran tried to hold on tight, but his teeth were old and very worn. His mouth slipped, and the boar broke free. The animal ran away into the dark, thick forest trees.

Cedric was very angry that the boar got away. He stomped his feet and shouted at poor Bran. "You are a useless dog!" he yelled. Bran felt very sad and quite ashamed.

Cedric raised his hand to scold the dog. Bran looked up at his master with soft, tired eyes. He wagged his tail slowly, hoping Cedric would remember all their happy years together.

"Master," Bran seemed to say, "my spirit is still brave, but my body is old. Do not forget the young dog I was while you look at the old dog today."

Cedric stopped and looked at his loyal friend. He remembered the many hunts they had won. He realized that Bran had given him all of his strength for many, many long years.

The moral is: Respect those who have served you well. We should value our friends for their past loyalty and kindness, even when they grow old and lose their youthful strength today.
