The Busy Body

Once, a happy group of friends lived together as one body. The most famous was Paunch the Belly. He was round, soft, and very quiet, sitting right in the center of everything.

Digit the Hands worked hard every single day. He lifted heavy objects, painted beautiful pictures, and picked delicious fruit. Digit felt very proud because he was always busy and moving until night.

Stride the Feet was also very busy. He carried the whole body up tall hills and across wide, green meadows. Stride never stopped moving, always walking or running to get everywhere on time.

Gnash the Mouth was the loudest of all. He chewed every snack and spoke every word. Gnash spent his days tasting sweet honey and crunchy apples, making sure the body enjoyed every bite.

One sunny day, Digit and Stride met to talk. "We do all the hard work," Digit whispered crossly. "I lift and carry while Stride walks miles. We are tired, but Paunch does nothing."

Gnash joined the secret meeting. "You are right!" Gnash shouted. "I chew and chew, but Paunch just sits there. He swallows everything I taste and never helps us. It is simply not fair."

The friends made a plan. Digit and Stride decided to stop working. "I will not lift any food," Digit declared. "And I will not walk to the kitchen," Stride added with a frown.

Paunch the Belly looked up, feeling very empty. "I am quite hungry," Paunch said softly. He looked at Digit, hoping for a snack. But Digit just crossed his fingers and looked away angrily.

Digit kept his promise and stayed perfectly still. He refused to pick up a single red apple or a crust of bread. Digit stood tall, watching Paunch grow smaller and much more quiet.

Stride refused to take even one step toward the garden. He stayed planted on the floor. "If Paunch wants to eat, he can go find food himself," Stride grumbled to the others.

Days passed, and something strange happened. Digit felt very weak and shaky. He could no longer lift his fingers. Stride felt heavy and tired, unable to stand up straight on the floor.

Gnash looked at Paunch and saw that the Belly was pale. Gnash tried to shout for help, but he had no breath left. Even Paunch looked sad and very thin without food.

Digit looked at Stride and realized the truth. "We need Paunch," Digit whispered weakly. "He takes the food we find and turns it into the strength we use to move and play daily."

Gnash quickly chewed a bite of bread for Paunch. Soon, the Belly was full again. Energy flowed back into Digit and Stride. They felt strong, fast, and happy to be working together.

All parts of a group are important. Even those who seem quiet do vital work. We must work together to stay strong. When one part suffers, the whole body feels the pain.
