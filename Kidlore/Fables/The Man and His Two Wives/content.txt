The Price of Pleasing Everyone

Once there lived a middle-aged man named Chronis. He lived in a very pretty cottage with his two lovely wives. Chronis was quite happy and felt very lucky to have such a family.

Chronis had hair that was both black and gray. He liked his hair very much. It made him look wise and youthful at the same time as he walked around his sunny garden.

His first wife, Vesper, was older and very kind. She wanted Chronis to look just like her. She did not like his dark hairs because they made him look too young and energetic.

Every single morning, Vesper sat with Chronis. She carefully used her small fingers to pull out every dark hair she found. She wanted him to be gray and wise like her own self.

His second wife, Thalassa, was much younger. She wanted Chronis to look youthful like her. She did not like his gray hairs because they made him look much too old and very tired.

Every single evening, Thalassa sat with Chronis. She used her quick fingers to pull out every gray hair she found. She wanted him to have dark hair just like her own beautiful hair.

Chronis wanted to make Vesper happy. He sat very still while she plucked his dark hair. He smiled even though it hurt a little bit to have his hair pulled out so often.

Vesper hummed a soft song while she worked. She pulled and pulled until the dark hairs were all gone. She felt happy seeing Chronis look more like a distinguished and very old man.

Chronis also wanted to make Thalassa happy. He sat very still while she plucked his gray hair. He wanted her to be glad and see him as a very young and strong man.

Thalassa laughed a bright song while she worked. She pulled and pulled until the gray hairs were all gone. She felt happy seeing Chronis look more like a youthful and very young man.

Day after day, the two wives kept plucking. Chronis let them do it because he did not want to say no. He thought pleasing everyone was the best way to live his life.

One sunny morning, Chronis went to the mirror. He wanted to see his handsome face and his hair. He looked very closely at his head but something felt very different and very strange.

Chronis shouted in surprise! His head was completely smooth and shiny. There were no dark hairs left and no gray hairs left. He was now a very bald man with no hair at all.

Vesper and Thalassa came into the room. They looked at his bald head and felt very sad. By trying to change him, they had lost the hair they both liked to touch everyday.

Chronis learned a lesson. If you try to please everyone, you might lose everything. It is better to be yourself. Yielding to every whim will only lead to a very big disaster.
