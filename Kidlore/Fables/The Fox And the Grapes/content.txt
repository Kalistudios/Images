Reynard and the High Grapes

Reynard the fox trotted through the golden forest. His tummy gave a loud, hungry rumble. He looked left and right for a tasty snack, hoping to find something sweet and very delicious today.

Suddenly, he saw a garden gate. Behind it sat a tall wooden trellis. Hanging from the top were the biggest, purple grapes he had ever seen. They looked juicy, cold, and perfectly ripe.

Pip the bluebird landed on the wooden fence. He chirped a happy greeting to the fox. Reynard licked his lips and pointed up at the purple fruit. "Those will be my lunch!" he declared.

Reynard crouched low to the soft grass. He wiggled his tail and focused his bright eyes on the highest bunch. He stretched his paws, getting ready for a very big, powerful, athletic jump.

With a mighty spring, Reynard launched himself into the air. He reached out his front paws as high as possible. But the grapes were much higher than he thought. He landed with a thump.

Pip hopped down to a lower branch. He tilted his head and watched the fox. The little bird flapped his wings and let out a soft tweet, wondering if his friend would try again.

"I just need a running start!" Reynard said. He backed up until he reached the edge of the garden. He took a deep breath and sprinted forward as fast as his legs could go.

Reynard leaped! He soared like a fluffy orange rocket. His claws brushed against a green vine, making the grapes dance and jingle. But he missed them again and landed flat on his belly.

Reynard looked around and found some old wooden crates. He carefully stacked one on top of another near the trellis. He climbed up slowly, balancing his paws to reach the sweet, purple fruit.

The crates began to wobble and shake. Reynard tried to stay still, but the top box slipped. He tumbled down into a pile of soft leaves, safe but feeling very tired and grumpy.

Pip flew down and sat beside the fox. Reynard looked up at the grapes one last time. He brushed the dust off his fur and tucked his tail behind him, looking very proud indeed.

"I do not want them anyway," Reynard huffed. "Those grapes are probably sour and very gross!" He walked away with his nose in the air, leaving the sweet grapes behind in the sun.
