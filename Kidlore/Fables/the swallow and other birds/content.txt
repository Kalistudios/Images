The Swallow and the Other Birds

Silas the hardworking farmer walked through his wide field, scattering tiny hemp seeds onto the dark, moist soil. Alkyon the Swallow watched him very carefully from her perch on a high tree branch.

Alkyon flew down to meet Corvus the Crow. She felt very worried about the new seeds. She knew that humans used hemp to make strong nets that could trap all the wild birds.

“Listen to me, Corvus!” Alkyon chirped loudly. “We must eat those seeds now. If they grow into plants, the farmer will use them to catch us in his heavy, scary nets one day.”

Corvus just shook his head and laughed at her. He thought Alkyon was being very silly. “Why worry about tiny seeds? I am far too busy looking for shiny treasures to even care now.”

Pip the Sparrow hopped over and chirped mockingly. He did not want to work or listen to warnings. He only wanted to play in the sunshine and eat the sweet, ripe, red berries.

“Please, my friends!” Alkyon pleaded. “If we do not stop this now, it will be too late. We must pick every seed from the ground before they start to grow very deep roots.”

Corvus and Pip ignored the wise swallow and flew away to the orchard. They left Alkyon alone in the field. She sighed and looked down at the freshly planted, very dangerous hemp seeds.

Soon, the rain fell and the warm sun shone. The tiny seeds began to sprout. Small, green leaves popped up through the dirt. Alkyon watched the field grow with a very heavy heart today.

Silas the farmer smiled as he looked at his healthy crop. The plants were growing taller every single day. He knew they would soon be ready to turn into some long, thick rope.

Alkyon visited Corvus one last time as the plants reached his knees. “Look at the hemp now!” she cried. “It is becoming the very thing that will soon take away our bird freedom forever.”

Corvus was too busy preening his dark feathers. He did not believe that a simple plant could ever hurt him. He told Alkyon to stop her worrying and leave him in peace today.

Silas harvested the tall plants. He worked hard to twist the fibers into a giant, strong net. He planned to catch many birds to keep them away from all his precious summer fruit.

Alkyon knew she could not save her friends. She flew away from the wild woods. She chose to live near the houses of humans where she felt much safer and stayed very calm.

One morning, a giant net fell over the hungry birds. Corvus and Pip struggled to escape, but the hemp was too strong. They finally realized that wise Alkyon had been right all along.

It is wise to listen to advice and stop trouble while it is small. Once a problem grows too big, it can be very difficult to fix. Always plan ahead for your tomorrow.
