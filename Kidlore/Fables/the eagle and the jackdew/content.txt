Jax and the Great Eagle

Altair the mighty Eagle soared through the bright blue sky. His golden eyes searched the green fields below for a snack. He was the strongest and fastest bird in the whole wide world.

Suddenly, Altair saw a small lamb near the river. He folded his wings and zoomed down like a fast rocket. With a huge splash of wind, he prepared to grab his tasty prize.

With his sharp talons, Altair gripped the woolly lamb. He flapped his powerful wings and lifted the animal high into the air. He made the difficult task look so very easy and simple.

Jax the small Jackdaw watched Altair from a wooden fence. He felt very jealous of the Eagle’s power. Jax wanted everyone to think that he was just as brave and strong too.

"I can do that!" Jax chirped loudly to the empty air. He fluffed his dark feathers and stretched his wings wide. He decided to find a huge animal to catch right now.

Jax spotted Crius the Ram grazing in the soft grass. Crius was very big and had very thick, curly wool. Jax thought catching a giant Ram would make him a hero today.

Jax flew high into the air, just like Altair did before. He wanted to copy the Eagle in every way. He circled the big Ram and got ready to make his move.

He closed his eyes and dived down toward the ground. He wanted to strike the big Ram with all his strength. He imagined the crowd cheering for the world’s most powerful Jackdaw.

Jax landed hard on the back of Crius the Ram. He dug his sharp little claws deep into the wool. But instead of flying away, Jax found that he was completely stuck.

Jax flapped his wings as hard as he could. He pulled and tugged, but the thick wool would not let go. He was trapped on top of the very large, heavy animal.

Crius the Ram looked over his shoulder at the noisy bird. He did not feel hurt at all. He just wondered why a tiny Jackdaw was dancing on his back so wildly.

Silas the Shepherd heard the loud flapping and came over to see. He saw Jax struggling and flapping. Silas reached out his big, gentle hands to catch the silly, tired little bird.

Silas untangled Jax from the wool and held him very tightly. He walked across the green field with the bird. He wanted to show the world what happens when you copy others.

Silas looked at the bird and smiled very warmly. Silas said, "You are just a Jackdaw, little friend. But in your own mind, you truly believed you were a mighty, flying Eagle."

Poor Jax learned a hard lesson that day. He realized that it is much better to be yourself than to try to be someone else. Do not let your pride trap you.
