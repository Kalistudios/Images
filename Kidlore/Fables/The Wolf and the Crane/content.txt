The Wolf and the Brave Crane

Lykos the wolf was very hungry today. He found a tasty piece of meat near the tall trees. He did not wait to chew it properly because he wanted to eat it all very fast.

Suddenly, Lykos started to cough. A sharp bone was stuck deep inside his throat. It hurt very much, and he could not swallow or breathe easily. He felt very scared and alone.

Lykos tried to shake his head. He tried to drink some water from the stream. Nothing worked to move the bone. He knew he needed someone with a very long, very thin neck.

He walked through the woods while holding his neck. He saw many animals, but none could help him. Finally, he reached a blue lake where a tall bird was standing very still.

Tsuru the crane was looking for fish in the water. She had very long legs and a very long beak. She looked very kind and peaceful under the bright, yellow morning sun.

Lykos went to Tsuru and pointed at his throat. He could not speak well, but he made her understand. He told her about the sharp bone that was making him feel bad.

"Please help me, Tsuru!" Lykos begged. "If you take the bone out, I will give you a giant reward. I have many treasures to share with a friend who helps me today."

Tsuru was very nervous. She knew that wolves could be dangerous and mean. She looked at the wolf's big teeth and wondered if she should trust his word and help him out.

Lykos opened his mouth as wide as he could. He stayed very still to show he would not bite. Tsuru took a deep breath and prepared to reach inside his dark mouth.

Tsuru put her long, graceful neck deep into the wolf's throat. It was very scary inside, but she was brave. She used her long beak to find the sharp, white bone.

With a quick pull, Tsuru grabbed the bone and lifted it out. Lykos felt the pain go away instantly. He was very happy to be able to breathe and swallow normally again.

Lykos jumped for joy and barked loudly at the sky. He felt strong and healthy once more. He forgot all about the terrible pain he felt just a few short minutes ago.

Tsuru stood before the wolf and waited. "Where is my reward?" she asked softly. "You promised me something special for saving your life today. I did exactly what you asked of me."

Lykos laughed a mean laugh. "You already have your reward," he said. "I let you put your head in my mouth and did not eat you. That is a very big gift!"

Tsuru flew away quickly. She learned a hard lesson that day. When you help a wicked person, do not expect a reward. Be glad if you escape without being hurt by them.
