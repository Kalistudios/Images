The Lion in Love

High upon a golden hill lived Leander, a mighty lion with a magnificent, flowing mane. He was the strongest king in the forest, and every single animal bowed low when he walked slowly by.

One sunny morning, Leander spotted a beautiful girl named Elara picking bright wildflowers near a small, wooden cottage. She sang a sweet song that touched the big lion’s heavy, golden heart in a special way.

Leander could not stop thinking about Elara's kind smile. He decided he wanted to marry her and live happily together. He felt a strange softness inside that he had never felt ever before.

Leander walked bravely to the cottage and spoke to Silas, the Woodman. "I love your daughter," Leander roared gently. "I wish to marry Elara. Please give me your blessing to be her dear husband."

Silas was very afraid of the giant lion. He did not want his daughter to marry a wild beast, but he was too scared to say "no" to such a powerful, mighty, hungry king.

Silas thought quickly and said, "Leander, my daughter is terrified of your very sharp teeth. If you pull them out, she will surely love you back and agree to be your lovely bride."

Because he was so blinded by love, Leander went to a healer. He had every one of his long, white teeth removed. He did not mind the pain because he truly wanted sweet Elara.

Leander returned to Silas with a gummy smile. "Look!" he lisped softly. "My teeth are gone. Now, may I marry Elara?" Silas looked at the lion and saw he was much less scary today.

"Wait," Silas said, pretending to be worried. "Elara is still frightened of your long, sharp claws. If you have them removed, she will finally feel safe enough to hold your big furry paw."

Leander wanted Elara more than anything else in the world. He went away and had all his sharp claws pulled out. Now, his paws were as soft and harmless as a tiny, little kitten's.

The lion returned to the cottage for a third time. He had no teeth and no claws left to protect himself. He felt very weak, but he was happy and hopeful deep inside.

Silas looked at the lion and realized he was no longer dangerous. The Woodman was not afraid anymore. He stood up tall and grabbed a heavy wooden club from behind his front door.

"Get away from here!" Silas shouted, waving his club. He chased the defenseless Leander into the deep woods. Without his teeth and claws, the poor lion could not fight back at all today.

Leander sat alone in the dark forest, feeling very foolish. He had given up his natural strength for a dream that was not real. He missed his teeth and his sharp claws dearly.

The moral is: never give up your natural strengths just to please others. Those who truly love you will always accept you exactly as you are, without ever asking you to change yourself.
