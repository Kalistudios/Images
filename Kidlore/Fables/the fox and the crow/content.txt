The Clever Fox and the Vain Crow

Bran the Crow soared through the bright morning sky. He spotted a delicious, golden block of cheese sitting on a picnic table. He swooped down and snatched the tasty prize with his beak.

Bran flew high up into a tall oak tree. He felt very lucky and proud. He settled on a sturdy branch, ready to eat his snack away from any other hungry forest animals.

Down on the soft forest floor, Flick the Fox was walking by. Suddenly, a wonderful smell drifted through the air. It was the scent of aged cheese, and Flick was very hungry.

Flick looked up and saw Bran perched high above. He saw the cheese held tightly in the crow's beak. Flick’s tummy rumbled loudly, and he knew he had to get that delicious snack.

Flick sat at the base of the tree and looked up. "Good morning, handsome bird!" he called out cheerfully. Bran looked down, holding the cheese firmly, but he did not say a word.

"I have never seen such shiny feathers," Flick said with a wide grin. "They sparkle like the night sky. You are surely the most elegant and beautiful creature in this whole green forest."

Bran tilted his head. He loved being told how beautiful he was. He puffed out his dark chest and flapped his wings slightly. He wanted to hear more of Flick's very kind words.

"If your voice is as lovely as your feathers," Flick continued, "then you must be the King of Birds. I wish I could hear you sing a song just for me today."

Bran was very vain. He wanted Flick to know that his voice was the best. He forgot all about the cheese in his beak as he prepared to show off his amazing talent.

Flick watched eagerly from below. He kept a very straight face, but inside, he was laughing. He moved closer to the tree trunk, waiting for the moment the yellow cheese would fall.

Bran opened his beak as wide as he could. "Caw! Caw!" he shouted loudly. As soon as he opened his mouth to sing, the heavy piece of cheese slipped out and fell down.

The cheese tumbled through the leaves. Bran watched in horror as his breakfast dropped. He tried to catch it, but he was too slow. Flick looked up with his bright, clever, sparkling eyes.

Flick jumped up and caught the cheese in his mouth before it hit the dirt. He swallowed the treat in one big gulp. It was the most delicious cheese he had ever tasted.

"Thank you for the song," Flick chuckled, looking up at the sad bird. "You have a voice, but you lack wit. Never trust those who flatter you only to get what they want."

The moral is to be careful of people who give too many compliments. They might be trying to trick you. True friends like you for who you are, and not for your prizes.
