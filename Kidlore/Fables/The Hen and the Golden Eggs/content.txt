The Farmer and the Magic Hen

Once there was a hardworking farmer named Gilder. He lived on a small farm with his favorite bird, a hen named Marigold. One sunny morning, he went to the barn to gather eggs.

Gilder looked inside Marigold’s nest and gasped. Instead of a white egg, he found a shiny, yellow one. It was very heavy and sparkled brightly. Gilder rubbed his eyes in total disbelief and wonder.

He looked at the egg closely. He realized it was pure, solid gold. Gilder was very happy. He ran back home to give Marigold some extra corn and some fresh, cool water.

The next morning, Gilder rushed to the barn. To his surprise, Marigold had laid another golden egg. He cheered and danced around the small coop. His little hen was truly a magical, wonderful bird.

Every single day, Marigold laid one beautiful golden egg. Gilder soon became the richest man in the land. He bought fine clothes and a big house. Life was very easy and very comfortable now.

However, Gilder became very greedy. He did not want to wait for just one egg each day. He wanted all the gold at once. He started to watch Marigold with very hungry, selfish eyes.

"She must be made of gold inside," Gilder thought. "If I open her up, I can get all the treasure right now." He stopped being kind to poor Marigold. He only thought of wealth.

Gilder forgot how lucky he was. He paced back and forth in his big house. He wanted to be even richer. He decided to find the pile of gold he imagined inside the hen.

Gilder walked slowly toward the barn. He carried a small sack in his hand. Marigold looked up at him with her soft, kind eyes. She did not know that her owner had changed inside.

He grabbed the poor hen. Marigold flapped her wings and tried to fly away. Gilder held her tight. He was only thinking about the gold. He did not care about his sweet, golden bird.

Gilder looked for the treasure. He wanted to see a mountain of gold. He thought the inside would be full of many bright, yellow coins, large bars, and many other very expensive, shiny things.

He was so wrong. There was no gold inside her at all. She was just a regular bird. Gilder realized his mistake. He had lost his special hen and his daily golden eggs forever.

Gilder sat on the ground and cried. He had no more gold and no more friend. He looked at the empty nest. He wished he could go back and be happy with just one egg.

He spent the rest of his days in a quiet house. He learned that being greedy only leads to losing everything. He was no longer rich. He was just a lonely man with nothing.

The moral of this story is that greed leads to ruin. If you want too much, you might lose what you already have. Be thankful for your daily blessings and do not be greedy.
