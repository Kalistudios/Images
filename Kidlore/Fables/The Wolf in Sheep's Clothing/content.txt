The Sneaky Guest in the Meadow

Lupin the wolf was very hungry. He watched the white sheep from behind a large rock. He wanted a tasty snack, but the careful shepherd and his big dogs kept the sheep safe.

"I must be clever," Lupin whispered to himself. He found an old, fuzzy sheepskin lying on the ground. He put it over his grey fur and looked just like a fluffy, white sheep.

Lupin walked slowly toward the meadow. He kept his head down and moved with a gentle bounce. The shepherd looked at the flock but did not see the hungry wolf hiding in the wool.

A little lamb named Bella walked over to Lupin. She thought he was a new friend. Bella shared some sweet, green grass with him. Lupin smiled, waiting for the sun to go down.

The sun began to set behind the hills. Silas the shepherd called the sheep back to the wooden fold. Lupin followed them quietly, hidden inside his warm, white disguise, feeling very sneaky and smart.

The gate clicked shut. Lupin was inside the fold with all the plump sheep. He found a dark corner and waited for the night to grow cold and the shepherd to go home.

Suddenly, the shepherd Silas came back. He was very hungry himself. He needed to find a sheep to provide a big dinner for his family. He walked into the fold with a lantern.

Silas looked around the crowded fold. He reached into the dark corner where Lupin was hiding. He grabbed the sheep that looked the biggest and heaviest of them all. It was sneaky Lupin!

The shepherd did not look closely at the wool. He lifted the wolf up high. Lupin tried to stay very still. He did not want to show his sharp teeth or his long tail.

Silas felt something strange. The wool was loose and the sheep felt very heavy. He pulled back the sheepskin. To his big surprise, he saw the grey fur of the sneaky, hungry wolf.

"Aha!" Silas cried out. He grabbed his staff and chased the wolf away. Lupin ran as fast as his legs could carry him, losing his sheepskin in the tall, prickly bushes near the gate.

Lupin was safe but very hungry. He learned a big lesson that night. It is never wise to pretend to be something you are not, because tricks can lead to big, scary trouble.
