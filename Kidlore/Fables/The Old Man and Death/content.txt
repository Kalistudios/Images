Theron’s Heavy Burden

Theron spent the whole day gathering dry sticks in the deep, green forest. He wanted to make a warm fire for his cozy little cottage before the cold night air arrived.

He tied the sticks together with a thick, sturdy rope. The bundle grew larger and larger until it was much taller than Theron himself. He took a very deep, long breath.

Theron hoisted the heavy load onto his bent back. He began the long walk home, his boots crunching on the dry leaves. The sun started to set, painting the sky orange.

The path became very steep and rocky. Theron’s knees began to shake, and his back started to ache. Every step felt like he was carrying a giant mountain on his tired shoulders.

Finally, Theron could not walk another single step. He let the heavy bundle slide off his back. It hit the ground with a loud thud, scattering a few small, dry twigs.

He sat down on a mossy log and groaned loudly. "Life is far too hard for an old man like me," Theron grumbled. He felt very tired and quite miserable indeed.

In a moment of great anger, Theron looked up at the darkening sky. "I am finished with this heavy burden!" he shouted. "Thanis, come and take me away from this toil!"

Suddenly, the wind began to howl through the trees. A thick, purple mist swirled around the forest path. The birds stopped singing, and everything became very quiet and very, very cold.

Out of the shadows stepped Thanis, the spirit of the end. He stood tall and silent, draped in a shimmering, dark robe that looked like the starry night sky itself.

Thanis looked down at the old man with eyes that glowed like soft moonlight. "You called my name, Theron," he whispered in a voice like rustling leaves. "Why am I here?"

Theron’s heart hammered against his ribs like a trapped bird. Seeing Thanis made him realize how much he loved the forest, the sun, and even his simple, cozy little wooden cottage.

He did not want to go. He wanted to see the morning sun again. Theron realized that his life, even with its heavy burdens, was a very precious and wonderful gift.

Theron quickly stood up and forced a nervous smile. "Oh, hello Thanis!" he stammered. "I am so glad you arrived. I was just wondering if you could help me with something."

Thanis tilted his head curiously. "I just needed a hand lifting this heavy bundle of sticks back onto my shoulders," Theron said quickly. "It is far too much for me alone."

Thanis helped him, then vanished into the mist. Theron walked home, happy to carry his load. Moral: We often complain about life, but we always want to keep living it.
