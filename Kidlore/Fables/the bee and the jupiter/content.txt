The Bee's Bitter Wish

Melissa the busy little bee lived in a bright field of flowers. One sunny morning, she decided to fly all the way up to the very top of high Mount Olympus today.

She carried a tiny golden pot filled with the sweetest honey ever made. Melissa wanted to give this special gift to great Jupiter, the king of all the mighty and powerful gods.

The journey was long, but Melissa flew past white clouds and blue skies. Finally, she reached the giant golden gates of the palace where the great god Jupiter lived in his glory.

Inside the palace, everything sparkled with bright light. Jupiter sat upon a massive throne made of pure gold. He looked down and saw the tiny bee waiting patiently for him to speak.

"Great Jupiter, I have brought you some honey," Melissa buzzed softly. She held out her golden pot. The honey smelled like summer flowers and sunshine, and it looked very delicious and sweet.

Jupiter took the tiny pot and tasted the honey. He smiled because it was the best honey he had ever eaten. The flavors were wonderful and made the great king very happy.

"This is a wonderful gift, Melissa," Jupiter said kindly. "Because you were so generous to me today, I will grant you one wish. Tell me, what is the one thing you want?"

Melissa thought about the humans who often took her honey. She was very selfish and did not want to share her hard work with anyone else. She wanted a way to stop them.

"Please give me a sharp sting," Melissa asked the king. "I want a weapon so powerful that it will kill any person who tries to take my sweet honey away from me."

Jupiter was very surprised by her request. He loved his people and did not want them to be hurt. He was upset that the little bee was being so mean and cruel.

Jupiter’s face became very stern and serious. He did not like the malice in Melissa’s heart. He decided to grant her wish, but it would come with a very big catch.

"I will give you the sting you ask for," Jupiter declared loudly. "It will be sharp and strong. But listen carefully, for this gift comes with a very heavy and sad price."

Melissa watched as Jupiter pointed his golden scepter at her. She felt a sharp new power inside her body. She was excited to finally have a way to protect her honey.

"If you ever use your sting to hurt someone, you will lose your own life," Jupiter warned. "The sting will stay in your enemy, and your life will end right then."

Be careful what you wish for, especially if it harms others. Selfishness and malice only bring trouble. It is always better to be kind and share your gifts with the world.
