The Tiny Guest and the Giant

In a bright, sunny meadow, a tiny gnat named Bree loved to fly through the warm summer air. She zipped and zoomed, feeling like she was the fastest little creature in the forest.

Nearby, a massive and very strong bull named Solon was quietly munching on some delicious green grass. He was so big that the trees seemed small whenever he walked past them today.

Bree flew circles around Solon, watching the giant animal move slowly. She wondered if the big bull would notice her. She decided to land on his head and take a very long rest.

With a gentle flap of her thin wings, Bree landed right on the tip of one of Solon’s great, curved horns. She settled down, making herself comfortable for a little while today.

Bree sat very still, thinking she was a heavy load for the bull. She worried that her extra weight was making it hard for Solon to graze normally on the green grass now.

Solon continued eating, completely unaware that a small guest was sitting on his head. He flicked his long tail to chase away flies, but he did not even feel the tiny gnat.

After a while, Bree felt ready to fly. She did not want to leave without saying goodbye to the bull who had carried her so very patiently for such a long time.

She flew down and buzzed loudly right next to Solon’s large, floppy ear. I am leaving now, Mr. Bull, Bree shouted. I hope my heavy weight did not make you feel tired.

Solon stopped eating and laughed. Oh, little one, I did not know you were there. I did not feel you land, and I certainly will not miss you as you fly away.

Moral: We often think we are more important to others than we actually are. It is wise to remember that our actions might not affect everyone as much as we imagine now.
