Barnaby and the Golden Goose

Barnaby was a poor man who lived in a small, humble cottage. He worked very hard every single day, but he never had enough money to buy the things he truly wanted or even needed.

One chilly morning, Barnaby found a beautiful, white goose in his garden. He decided to keep her as a pet. He gave the goose some corn and a soft bed of dry, yellow hay.

The next day, Barnaby went to check on the goose. He saw something shiny in her nest. It was a bright, heavy egg made of solid gold! Barnaby could not believe his own eyes.

Barnaby ran to the village and sold the golden egg for a lot of money. He bought delicious food and new clothes. He felt like the luckiest man in the whole wide world today.

Every single morning, the goose laid another golden egg. Barnaby became very wealthy quite quickly. He built a big house and filled it with fine furniture. He was finally a very rich man.

As Barnaby got richer, he also became very greedy. He did not want to wait every day for just one egg. He wanted all the gold right now to buy even more expensive things.

Barnaby watched the goose closely. He thought there must be a giant mountain of gold hidden inside her. He imagined millions of shiny golden eggs just waiting for him to reach in and grab.

"If I open her up, I will get all the gold at once," Barnaby whispered to himself. His eyes sparkled with greed. He forgot how kind the goose had been to him lately.

The goose looked at Barnaby with her gentle eyes. She did not know what he was planning. Barnaby picked up a sharp tool. He was thinking only about the gold inside the bird.

Barnaby caught the goose and held her tight. He was so excited to become the richest man on earth. He did not care about the poor bird. He just wanted the shiny gold.

With one quick movement, Barnaby cut the goose open. He reached inside, expecting to find piles of gold. But to his great surprise, there was no gold at all inside the white bird.

The goose was just a normal goose on the inside. She was gone forever, and she would never lay another golden egg again. Barnaby stood there in his empty kitchen, feeling very foolish.

Barnaby started to cry. He had lost his wonderful friend and his source of wealth. He realized that his greed had destroyed everything. He was rich, but now he felt very, very poor.

He looked at the empty nest where the golden eggs used to be. There would be no more shiny surprises. Barnaby learned a very hard lesson that day about being patient and thankful.

The moral of the story is: those who want too much often lose everything. Greed can make you lose the good things you already have. Always be grateful for what you are given.
