The Loyal Guard and the Meat

Phylax was a very brave watchdog. He lived in a grand house with a tall fence. Every night, he sat by the gate to keep his home safe while everyone inside was sleeping.

Near the garden, a man named Dolon was hiding in the bushes. He was a thief who wanted to sneak inside. He wore a dark coat and carried a heavy bag of tools.

Dolon watched the golden windows of the house. He waited for the lights to go out. He thought he could sneak past the dog, but Phylax had very sharp ears and a nose.

Phylax smelled something strange in the cool night air. He stood up and pointed his ears toward the dark trees. He knew that someone was hiding nearby, and he stayed very, very alert.

Dolon stepped out from the shadows. He walked toward the iron gate. He looked at the big dog and felt a little bit scared. He knew he had to be very clever today.

When Phylax saw the stranger, he let out a low, rumbling growl. He bared his teeth to show that he was ready to protect his family. He would not let any stranger enter.

Dolon reached into his deep pocket and pulled out a large, juicy piece of meat. It smelled delicious. He thought the dog would be so hungry that he would forget his important job.

"Here, good boy," Dolon whispered with a sneaky smile. He tossed the meat over the fence. The treat landed on the grass right in front of the dog’s paws. He waited for Phylax.

Phylax looked down at the tasty meat. His tummy was empty, but his heart was full of loyalty. He did not eat the food. He looked back up at the man with anger.

"Why don't you eat?" Dolon asked very softly. "I am your friend. I brought this tasty gift just for you. Please, take the meat and let me come inside the gate now."

Phylax barked so loudly that the birds flew away from the trees. He stood tall and firm. He would not move an inch. He knew that the man was not a real friend.

"I know your trick," Phylax barked. "You give me food now so you can steal everything later. If I take your gift, I would be failing my masters. I am a loyal dog."

Dolon realized that his plan had failed completely. He could not bribe the honest dog. He saw the lights in the house turning on. He knew he had to leave the garden quickly.

The thief turned around and ran back into the dark forest. He dropped his heavy bag and did not look back. Phylax watched him go, knowing that the house was safe again.

The moral of the story is that a bribe is often a trap. Be careful when people give you gifts to make you forget your duty. Loyalty is more valuable than any treat.
