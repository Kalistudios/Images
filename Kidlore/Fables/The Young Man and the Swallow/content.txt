Caspian and the Early Bird

Caspian is a young man who loves shiny things. He spends all his gold on toys and sweets. He never thinks about tomorrow, only about having the most fun right this moment.

Caspian’s big house became very empty. He had sold his chairs, his tables, and even his soft bed. Now, he only had his heavy, warm winter coat to keep him very cozy.

One chilly morning, Caspian walked outside. The ground was still frozen and white. He shivered in his thick, woolly coat, wishing for the bright sun and the warm, green grass of summertime.

High above the snowy hills, a small bird named Skylar was flying fast. Skylar was a brave swallow who had returned early. He chirped loudly, flapping his wings against the cold, biting wind.

Caspian looked up and saw Skylar zooming through the blue sky. His eyes went wide with excitement. He had always heard that swallows only come back when the long, cold winter is over.

"Look!" Caspian shouted to the empty trees. "The swallow is here! That means summer has arrived today. I do not need this heavy, itchy old coat anymore. I can finally be free and light."

Caspian ran quickly to the village market. He found a merchant and traded his warm coat for a small bag of silver coins. He felt very clever for getting money for old clothes.

With his new silver, Caspian bought a delicious, cold ice cream and a colorful silk shirt. He walked through the park, licking his treat and feeling very fancy in the bright, thin fabric.

Skylar the swallow landed on a nearby fence. He tucked his head under his wing, trying to stay warm. The little bird was very tired and confused by the lingering, icy morning frost.

Suddenly, the bright sun disappeared behind dark, heavy clouds. The wind began to howl through the trees. Large, white snowflakes started to fall from the sky, covering the green grass in cold ice.

Caspian started to shake. His thin silk shirt did not protect him from the freezing storm. He missed his heavy coat very much. He realized that the warm summer had not actually arrived.

He looked for Skylar on the fence. The poor little swallow was curled into a tiny ball, shivering in the snow. Skylar had also been fooled by the single day of bright, yellow sunshine.

Caspian picked up the cold bird and tucked him into his pocket. "I am so sorry, Skylar," he whispered. "I was too greedy and too fast. We both expected summer way too soon."

They huddled together under a porch, waiting for the real spring to come. Caspian learned that one swallow does not make a summer. He promised to be much wiser with his choices tomorrow.

We must learn to be patient and look at the whole picture. Do not rush into big decisions based on just one small sign. Good things come to those who wait for maturity.
