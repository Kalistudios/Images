Regulus and the Lion's Share

Regulus the mighty Lion prowled across the golden grass. He met Vulpis the clever Fox near a tall tree. They decided to hunt together so they could catch a very big, tasty meal.

Soon, they saw Anubis the Jackal resting by a rock. Regulus told him about the plan. Anubis was very hungry and agreed to help the others find some food in the wide valley.

The three friends walked quietly through the tall, yellow weeds. Vulpis and Anubis sniffed the air for a trail. They wanted to find a large stag to share for their big dinner.

Regulus spotted a grand stag near the blue river. He whispered a plan to Vulpis. The Fox would chase the animal toward the bushes where the big Lion was waiting to jump out.

Anubis barked loudly to start the chase. The stag ran fast, but Regulus was much faster. With a great leap and a mighty roar, the Lion caught the prize for the hunting team.

They gathered around the meal. Vulpis looked at the meat and felt very hungry. Regulus stood tall and began to divide the food into four equal parts for the group to eat today.

Regulus pointed to the first portion. He said he must take this share because he is the King of the forest. Vulpis nodded slowly, knowing that the King always gets his way.

Then, Regulus looked at the second portion. He claimed it for himself because he was the strongest hunter. Anubis watched the Lion with big eyes, feeling a little bit worried about his dinner.

Regulus touched the third portion with his heavy paw. He said this part belonged to him because he led the hunt today. Vulpis sighed and realized there was very little food left.

Finally, Regulus glared at the last portion of meat. He told Anubis that anyone who touched it would have to fight him. The Jackal backed away quickly, feeling very scared of him.

Vulpis and Anubis walked away with empty tummies. They were sad because they had worked hard but had nothing to eat. They learned that some partners do not like to share their things.

Regulus ate the whole meal all by himself. He was full, but he was also very alone. He sat under the sun, a powerful King with no friends left to hunt him.
