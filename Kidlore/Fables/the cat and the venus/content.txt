The Cat and the Golden Wish

Once there lived a very graceful cat named Elara with soft fur and bright eyes. She spent her days watching a handsome man named Galen who lived in the house across the garden.

Elara the cat loved Galen so much that she wished she could be a human woman. She wanted to walk beside him, talk to him, and share a very happy life with him.

One night, Elara prayed to the kind goddess Venus. She begged the goddess to change her into a beautiful lady so that Galen could finally fall in love with her very soon.

Venus heard the cat's prayer and felt sorry for her. With a bright flash of golden light, Elara was transformed into a stunningly beautiful young woman with a very lovely, sweet smile.

When Galen saw the beautiful woman, he was amazed by her grace. He did not know she was really his cat, and he fell deeply in love with her right away today.

Soon, Galen asked the beautiful maiden to marry him. They had a wonderful wedding ceremony filled with music, flowers, and many friends, and everyone cheered for the happy, handsome and young couple.

The new husband and wife lived in a grand house together. Elara tried very hard to act like a proper lady, sitting quietly and wearing the most elegant and colorful silk dresses.

Venus watched the couple from the clouds above. She wondered if Elara had truly changed her inner nature, or if she was still just a cat hidden inside a human body today.

To test Elara, Venus decided to play a small trick. She quietly let a tiny, brown mouse run across the floor of the room while the couple was resting together very peacefully.

As soon as Elara saw the small mouse scurrying by, she forgot she was a human. Her eyes grew wide and her instincts took over her mind and her whole furry body.

She jumped out of her soft bed and began to chase the mouse around the room. She scrambled over the furniture and dived under the tables, just like a hungry, hunting cat.

With a quick pounce, Elara caught the little mouse in her hands. She was very proud of her catch, but she did not realize that Galen was watching her very strangely today.

Venus was very disappointed to see that Elara's character had not changed. Even though she looked like a human, her heart and her habits were still those of a very hungry cat.

The goddess waved her hand and turned Elara back into a cat. Galen was shocked to see his beautiful wife disappear, leaving only his pet cat sitting on the cold floor alone.

The moral of the story is that you can change your appearance, but your true nature remains. It is very difficult to change the way you really are deep down inside yourself.
