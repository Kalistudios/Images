The Bear and the Best Friends

Galen and Pere were two very close friends who decided to take a long journey together through a wild, green forest. They promised to always look out for each other no matter what.

The sun was shining brightly as the two travelers walked along the winding dirt path. They laughed and talked about their big adventures, feeling very safe and happy in each other’s friendly company.

Suddenly, a massive, growling bear stepped out from behind a very large oak tree. The beast looked hungry and fierce, its dark eyes fixed on the two terrified friends standing in the middle.

Pere was a very fast climber. Without saying a single word to his friend Galen, he grabbed a low branch and pulled himself high up into the safety of a leafy green tree.

Galen was left all alone on the ground. He knew he was not fast enough to climb a tree or run away. He felt very scared as the huge bear walked slowly toward him.

Galen remembered hearing that bears will not touch a person who is already dead. He quickly fell to the cold ground and held his breath, lying as still as a heavy grey stone.

The huge bear reached Galen and began to sniff around his ears and nose. Galen kept his eyes tightly shut and did not move a single muscle, even though his heart was racing.

The bear sniffed Galen’s head for a long time. It seemed to be whispering something into his ear. Finally, the bear decided that the man was not alive and walked quietly back away.

Once the bear was completely gone, Pere climbed down from the high branches of the tree. He brushed the dust off his clothes and walked over to where his friend was still lying.

Pere gave a little laugh, trying to act like he had not been scared at all. He reached out a hand to help Galen up, but Galen stayed on the grass for now.

"That was a very close call!" Pere said loudly. "I saw the bear whispering in your ear just now. Tell me, dear friend, what did the big beast have to say to you?"

Galen slowly stood up and looked at Pere with very sad eyes. He realized that his friend had left him behind when things got dangerous, which made his heart feel very heavy today.

"The bear gave me some very good advice," Galen replied quietly while brushing dirt off his coat. "He told me something important about the people we choose to travel with on our long journeys."

Galen looked straight at Pere and said, "The bear told me never to travel with a friend who deserts you and runs away the very moment that danger comes near to you both."

Moral: Misfortune is the true test of friendship. A real friend will stay by your side during hard times. Never trust those who leave you behind when you are in a scary situation.
