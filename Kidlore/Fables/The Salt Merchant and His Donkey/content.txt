The Clever Donkey's Lesson

Nikos was a hardworking merchant who lived in a small village. He owned a very strong donkey named Jago. Together, they traveled many long miles every single day to sell goods at the market.

One morning, Nikos loaded several heavy bags of salt onto Jago’s back. The salt was very heavy, and Jago groaned. He did not like carrying such a big load under the hot morning sun.

To reach the market, they had to cross a shallow river. The water was cool and clear. Nikos walked carefully on the stones, while Jago followed behind him with the heavy bags of salt.

Suddenly, Jago slipped on a wet rock and fell into the river. Splash! He struggled to stand up in the water. Nikos rushed to help his friend get back onto his four feet.

When Jago stood up, he felt very surprised. The heavy bags of salt had dissolved in the water! The load was now light as a feather. Jago felt very happy and truly refreshed.

Nikos was disappointed because the salt was gone. They had to return home with nothing to sell. Jago trotted back quickly, feeling very clever for finding a way to lighten his heavy load.

The next day, Nikos loaded more salt onto Jago. As they reached the river, Jago remembered what happened before. He decided to fall into the water on purpose to lose the heavy weight.

Jago tumbled into the river again. Just like before, the salt melted away. Jago stood up easily and looked at Nikos. He was very pleased with his trick. The load was finally gone.

Nikos realized that Jago was playing a trick on him. He was a very wise man and knew how to teach Jago a lesson. He decided to change the load tomorrow morning.

On the third day, Nikos did not pack salt. Instead, he filled the bags with many dry sponges. They were very light, and Jago was happy as they walked to the river.

When they reached the water, Jago smiled. He thought he would make the load even lighter. He jumped into the river with a big splash, waiting for the bags to disappear once again.

But something went wrong! The sponges did not melt. Instead, they soaked up all the river water. The bags became ten times heavier than they were before. Jago could hardly even move.

Jago struggled and panted as he tried to stand up. The heavy, wet sponges pulled him down. Nikos watched him, waiting for Jago to learn that his tricks would not work anymore.

Jago finally dragged himself out of the water. He was very tired and soaking wet. He had to carry the heavy, wet sponges all the way to the market without any complaining.

Jago never tried to trick Nikos again. He learned that being lazy often makes things harder. If you try to avoid your work, you might end up with a much heavier burden indeed.
