Vesper and the Great Forest Feud

Deep in the ancient forest, a great trouble began to grow. The proud Beasts of the earth and the colorful Birds of the sky gathered for a giant battle over the golden valley.

Ursus the mighty bear led the heavy Beasts. They sharpened their claws and practiced their loudest roars. They were sure that their strength would win the day against the light, feathered Birds above.

Aquila the eagle led the swift Birds. They flapped their powerful wings and soared through the clouds. They believed their speed and sharp eyes would help them beat the heavy Beasts on land.

Vesper the bat watched the two armies from his cave. He was very nervous and did not know which side to join. He waited to see who would be the strongest in battle.

The battle began with a great noise. At first, the Beasts seemed much stronger. Ursus pushed the Birds back. Vesper quickly flew down to the ground to stand beside the heavy, furry animals.

"I am a Beast like you!" Vesper cried to Ursus. "Look at my sharp teeth and my soft fur. I belong on the ground with the winners." The Beasts cheered for their new friend.

But the wind changed, and the Birds began to fly higher and strike faster. Soon, the Beasts were running away. Aquila and the Birds were winning the long fight for the valley floor.

Vesper saw the Birds winning and changed his mind quickly. He left the Beasts and flew up into the sky. He wanted to be on the winning side before the sun went down.

"I am a Bird!" Vesper told Aquila. "Look at my wide wings and how well I can fly. I belong in the sky with you." The Birds let him join their flying army.

Finally, Ursus and Aquila decided to stop fighting. The Birds and Beasts made a special peace treaty. They realized they did not want to fight anymore and shook paws and wings together happily.

Vesper tried to celebrate with everyone, but they turned away. "You are not a true friend," they said. "You only joined whoever was winning." Both groups told the dishonest bat to go away.

Poor Vesper was all alone. He had no friends on the ground or in the sky. Now he only comes out at night, hiding in the dark shadows where no one can see his face.
