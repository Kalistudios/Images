The Man and the Satyr

Theron walked through the deep, white snow in the middle of a very cold forest. The wind howled loudly around him, and he pulled his heavy cloak tighter to stay warm today.

The traveler’s fingers were becoming very stiff from the freezing air. Theron shivered and looked around for any place to rest, but he only saw tall trees and thick, icy snow everywhere.

Nearby, a curious Satyr named Silvan was watching from behind a large pine tree. Silvan had the legs of a goat and small horns on his head, and he looked very surprised.

Silvan stepped out into the open to greet the cold traveler. "You look frozen, human," the Satyr said kindly. He offered to lead Theron to his warm cave for some hot food.

As they walked, Theron lifted his hands to his mouth and blew a warm breath onto his fingers. He did this several times as the cold wind continued to blow through the trees.

Silvan watched him with a confused look. He had never seen a human do that before. "Why are you blowing on your hands like that, my new friend?" the curious Satyr asked.

"My hands are very cold," Theron explained to the Satyr. "I am blowing on them to warm them up so that I can feel my fingers again in this very bitter winter."

Silvan nodded, though he still looked a bit puzzled by the man’s actions. Soon, they reached the Satyr’s cozy home, which was tucked away safely inside a large, dry, and warm cave.

Inside the cave, a bright fire was crackling happily. Silvan invited Theron to sit down on a wooden stool while he prepared a big bowl of thick, hot porridge for his guest.

The porridge was steaming and very hot. Silvan handed the bowl to Theron, who was very hungry. The traveler held the bowl carefully and leaned down toward the hot, delicious-smelling food.

Theron began to blow gently on the spoonful of porridge. He did this again and again before taking a small bite. Silvan watched him closely, his eyes growing very wide with confusion.

"What are you doing now?" Silvan asked. "Is the food not warm enough for you? Why are you blowing on your porridge in the same way you blew on your cold fingers?"

"The porridge is much too hot," Theron replied calmly. "I am blowing on it now to cool it down so that I do not burn my mouth while I am eating it."

Silvan jumped up in anger. "Go away!" he shouted. "I will not trust a man who blows both hot and cold from the very same mouth. You must leave my home immediately!"

Moral: Avoid those who are inconsistent or "blow hot and cold." A person who says different things to suit the moment cannot be trusted. Always be wary of double-faced and fickle people.
