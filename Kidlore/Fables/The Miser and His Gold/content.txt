The Secret in the Field

Silas was a man who loved gold more than anything else. He spent every hour counting his coins. He never bought food or clothes because he wanted to keep every single penny.

Silas worked hard, but he never enjoyed his wealth. He lived in a cold house with very little furniture. To Silas, the only thing that mattered was seeing his shiny, yellow gold grow.

One day, Silas decided to sell every single thing he owned. He sold his house, his bed, and his pots. He used all the money to buy one giant, heavy lump of gold.

The gold nugget was the most beautiful thing Silas had ever seen. It was heavy and bright. He held it close to his chest and smiled a very greedy, happy smile today.

Silas did not want anyone to see his treasure. He found a secret spot in a lonely field far away. He made sure that no one was watching him in the tall grass.

Silas used a small shovel to dig a deep hole in the ground. He placed the heavy gold nugget inside the dark earth. He covered it up with dirt and some rocks.

Every morning, Silas walked to the secret spot. He wanted to make sure his gold was still there. He would dig it up just to look at its bright, shimmering, golden surface.

Silas never used the gold to buy anything. He just looked at it and felt rich. Then, he would bury it again and walk home, feeling very satisfied with his hidden, secret wealth.

Silas did this every day for a long time. The gold stayed in the ground, doing nothing at all. Silas was happy just knowing that his precious treasure was safe and sound.

However, a sneaky thief named Bram had been watching Silas from behind a large oak tree. Bram saw Silas digging every day. He wondered what was hidden so deeply inside the quiet field.

That night, when Silas was sleeping, Bram went to the hole. He dug up the gold and ran away quickly. He left nothing behind but a very dark and empty hole.

The next morning, Silas arrived at the spot. He started to dig, but his hands found nothing but dirt. His gold was gone! Silas began to scream and pull his gray hair.

Silas wailed so loudly that a neighbor named Julian came running to see what was wrong. Silas cried, "My gold is gone! Someone stole my life!" He pointed at the empty, dark hole.

Julian looked at Silas and said, "Why be sad? Just put a stone in the hole. It is the same thing, because you never used the gold when you had it."

Silas realized the truth. Wealth is only useful if it is used for good. The moral of the story is: wealth that is not used is the same as having no wealth.
