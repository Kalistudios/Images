The Tortoise and the Hare

In a bright green forest, a fast Hare loved bragging about his speed. He laughed loudly at other animals and proudly claimed nobody in the whole forest could ever beat him in a race.

Nearby, a calm Tortoise listened quietly while the Hare continued boasting. The other animals felt annoyed, but the Tortoise simply smiled kindly and stayed peaceful without saying anything rude back to him.

One sunny morning, the Hare shouted, “I am the fastest animal alive!” The Tortoise slowly stepped forward and surprised everyone by saying, “I would like to challenge you to a race.”

The forest animals gasped in surprise and then burst into laughter. The Hare laughed the loudest and happily accepted the challenge, feeling completely sure he would easily win the race.

Soon, the Fox marked a long path through the forest with stones and leaves. All the animals gathered excitedly near the starting line, ready to watch the unusual race begin.

“Ready, set, go!” shouted the Fox loudly. The Hare dashed forward like lightning while the Tortoise began walking slowly and carefully, taking steady little steps along the forest path without stopping.

The Hare quickly moved far ahead and looked back with a grin. Seeing the Tortoise still walking slowly behind him, he laughed proudly and decided he had plenty of extra time left.

Feeling overconfident, the Hare found a shady tree beside the path. “I can rest for a little while,” he said lazily before stretching out comfortably and falling fast asleep under the tree.

Meanwhile, the Tortoise kept moving forward step by step. He never stopped walking, never looked back, and stayed focused on reaching the finish line despite the Hare’s teasing and laughter earlier.

After a long time, the Tortoise quietly passed the sleeping Hare. The forest animals watched silently, amazed by the Tortoise’s patience, determination, and steady effort throughout the important race competition.

At last, the Tortoise crossed the finish line while the animals cheered loudly with joy. Suddenly, the Hare woke up and ran quickly, but sadly he was already far too late.

The Hare felt embarrassed and understood his mistake. The wise Tortoise smiled kindly as the animals celebrated together, remembering forever that slow, steady effort and patience can sometimes defeat careless confidence.
