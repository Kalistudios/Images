The Forest’s Foolish Gift

Axel the woodsman walked deep into the emerald forest. He carried a heavy, sharp axe head made of cold iron, but it had no handle to hold. He needed wood very desperately.

Axel stopped before Sylvan, the oldest and mightiest Oak tree in the woods. Sylvan’s branches reached toward the clouds, and his trunk was wide and strong. Axel looked up and spoke softly.

"Kind Sylvan," Axel whispered, "I only need one small piece of wood. If you grant me a handle for my tool, I shall leave the rest of this beautiful forest alone forever."

Sylvan looked down at his fellow trees. He felt very proud and safe in his grove. He did not think a tiny piece of wood could cause any harm to his kingdom.

Sylvan called to young Asher, a slender Ash tree growing nearby. "Do not worry, little one," Sylvan boomed. "Give this man a sturdy branch so he may go on his way quickly."

Asher was young and flexible. He offered a straight, strong limb to Axel. The young tree was happy to help the great Sylvan and felt very brave for serving the forest elders.

Axel took the branch and began to carve it with a small knife. He whistled a low tune as the wood became smooth. Finally, he wedged it tightly into his iron axe.

Now Axel had a complete weapon. He gripped the new handle firmly. It fit his hand perfectly, thanks to Asher’s gift. He swung the axe through the air with a loud whoosh.

Instead of leaving, Axel turned toward Sylvan. With his new handle, he struck the mighty Oak with a powerful blow. The forest echoed with the sharp, biting sound of the cold iron.

Sylvan groaned in pain as the sharp blade bit deep into his bark. He realized too late that by giving away the little tree, he had brought ruin upon the entire forest.

Axel chopped and chopped, his new handle holding strong. One by one, the noble trees began to fall. The forest was no longer a safe home, but a place of great sorrow.

The trees learned a hard lesson. If you sacrifice your neighbors to save yourself, you will soon face the same fate. We must protect one another to keep our world safe.
