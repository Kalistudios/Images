The Monkey and the Dolphin

Caper the Monkey sails on a big ship. He loves looking at the deep blue sea. He imagines he is a brave explorer, even though he is just a very small passenger.

Suddenly, a giant storm hits the boat! The waves are tall and the wind is very loud. The boat begins to sink, and poor Caper falls right into the cold, salty water.

Caper struggles to stay afloat. He cannot swim very well and feels very scared. Just then, a friendly dolphin named Echo swims to the surface to help the drowning monkey stay alive.

Echo carries Caper toward the shore. The sun starts to shine again. Echo is happy to help a traveler. She wonders where this furry little friend has come from on his long journey.

"Are you from Athens?" Echo asks kindly. Caper wants to sound very important and wise. He does not want to admit he is lost. "Yes, I am a very famous prince there!"

Echo is impressed. She swims faster. "Then you must know Piraeus," she says. She is talking about the famous port. Caper thinks Piraeus is the name of a person or a king.

"Oh, Piraeus?" Caper shouts with a wide grin. "He is one of my very best friends! We eat dinner together every single week. He is a very tall and very rich man."

Echo stops swimming immediately. She looks very confused and a bit sad. She knows that Piraeus is a place, not a person. She realizes that Caper is telling her a big lie.

"Piraeus is the name of a harbor, not a man!" Echo explains. Caper turns very red. He realizes his silly mistake. He tries to change his story, but it is too late.

Echo sighs deeply. She does not like to be lied to. She feels that if someone is dishonest about small things, they cannot be trusted with big things. She stops moving forward.

"I will help you to the shore," Echo says, "but you must learn that lying does not make you important. It only makes you look foolish. Truth is always the best path."

Caper reaches the sandy beach safely. He watches Echo swim away into the distance. He promises himself to never lie again, especially just to sound important. Honesty is the best policy. Moral: Lies bring trouble.
