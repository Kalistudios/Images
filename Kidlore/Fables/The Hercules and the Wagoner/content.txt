Theron and the Mighty Hero

Theron was a simple man driving his heavy wagon along a very muddy country road. He whistled a happy tune as his two strong horses pulled the wooden cart toward the village market.

Suddenly, the sky turned dark and a heavy rain began to fall. The dirt road quickly turned into thick, sticky mud. The wagon wheels began to sink deeper and deeper into the ground.

With a loud crack, the back wheel hit a deep rut. The horses pulled hard, but the wagon did not move. Theron jumped down from his seat into the cold, wet mud.

Theron looked at the stuck wheel and sighed. Instead of trying to push the heavy wagon himself, he sat down on a dry rock. He felt very tired and quite helpless today.

"Oh, great Hercules!" Theron shouted toward the stormy clouds. "Please come down from the high mountains and help me move this heavy wagon. I cannot do this difficult and muddy work alone."

Suddenly, a bright light shone through the gray clouds. The mighty hero Hercules appeared before Theron. He stood tall and strong, looking down at the lazy wagoner with a very stern face.

Theron looked up at the brave hero with wide, hopeful eyes. He expected Hercules to lift the wagon with one hand. He waited for a miracle to happen in the messy mud.

Hercules did not touch the wagon. Instead, he folded his powerful arms. He looked at the muddy wheels and then he looked back at Theron, who was still sitting on the rock.

"Get up, man!" Hercules commanded with a loud, booming voice. "Put your shoulder to the wheel and whip your horses. Do not just sit there crying for help without trying your best."

Theron felt ashamed of his laziness. He stood up and walked to the stuck wheel. He leaned his shoulder against the wood and prepared to push as hard as he possibly could.

Theron pushed with all his might while the horses pulled. Slowly, the wheel turned. The wagon rolled out of the deep mud. Theron realized he was much stronger than he had thought.

Hercules smiled and faded away. The moral is that the gods help those who help themselves. You must work hard and try your best before you ask others for their help.
