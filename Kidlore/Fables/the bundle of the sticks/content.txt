The Strength of the Bundle

Theron was an old, wise man who lived on a peaceful farm. He worked hard every day, but his heart was heavy with worry for his two young sons, Soren and Bram.

Soren and Bram were strong boys, but they could never agree on anything. They argued about the sheep, the garden, and even the weather. Their loud shouting filled the quiet valley every day.

One sunny afternoon, the brothers were fighting over a wooden bucket. Soren pulled one way, and Bram pulled the other. Theron watched from the porch, feeling very sad about their constant bickering.

Theron decided it was time to teach his sons a very important lesson. He called out to them in a loud, firm voice. "Soren, Bram! Come here and listen to your old father."

The brothers stopped their fighting and walked over to Theron. He asked Soren to go into the forest and gather a large pile of thin, dry sticks. Soren did as he was told.

When Soren returned, Theron sat down on a log. He took the sticks and tied them together tightly with a thick, rough rope. He made sure the bundle was very firm and solid.

Theron handed the heavy bundle of sticks to his oldest son, Soren. "I want you to try your very best to break these sticks in half," Theron said with a serious, calm look.

Soren gripped the bundle tightly. He used all his strength, pushing and pulling with his powerful arms. His face turned bright red, but the bundle did not break or even bend a bit.

"It is impossible, Father!" Soren grumbled, handing the bundle back. Theron then turned to Bram. "Now it is your turn, my son. Show me if you are stronger than this bundle of wood."

Bram tried even harder than his brother. He leaned his knee against the sticks and pulled with a loud grunt. No matter how much he struggled, the bundle stayed together in one piece.

Both brothers were frustrated and confused. They could not understand why their father was asking them to do such a hard task. Theron smiled gently and began to untie the thick, brown rope.

Theron took the bundle apart and gave one single stick to Soren and one single stick to Bram. "Now, my sons, try to break these single pieces of wood," he said very quietly.

Snap! Soren broke his stick easily with just one hand. Snap! Bram broke his stick just as fast. The pieces of wood fell to the dusty ground. The brothers looked at each other.

"My sons," Theron said, "when you are together like the bundle, no one can break you. But when you fight and stand alone, you are weak and easily broken. You must stay united."

The moral of the story is that there is great strength in unity. When we work together and support one another, we are much stronger than we could ever be all alone.
