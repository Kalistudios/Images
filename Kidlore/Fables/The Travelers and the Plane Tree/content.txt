The Gift of the Shade

Long ago, two best friends named Arlo and Bram set out on a very long journey. They carried heavy bags and wore sturdy boots, walking across the dry, dusty land under the hot sun.

The golden sun was very hot that afternoon. Arlo wiped his brow while Bram sighed. There was no water nearby, and the heat made their tired legs feel very heavy and quite slow.

Suddenly, Arlo pointed his finger toward a tall, green shape in the distance. It was a giant Plane Tree named Elden. His wide branches spread out like a big, leafy, green umbrella.

"Look over there!" Bram shouted with joy. They both ran as fast as they could toward the giant tree. The dusty path felt long, but the promise of cool shade kept them moving.

Arlo and Bram finally reached the thick trunk of Elden. They dropped their heavy bags on the soft grass. The air under the tree was cool, fresh, and much better than the sun.

They lay down on the ground, looking up through the many layers of green leaves. The hot sun could not reach them here. Arlo closed his eyes and took a very deep breath.

After resting for a while, Arlo began to look closely at the tall branches above him. He looked for something to eat. He searched for bright red apples or sweet, purple, juicy grapes.

"This tree has no fruit at all," Arlo grumbled loudly. He felt grumpy now that he was cool. He poked at the bark and frowned because there was nothing sweet for him to eat.

Bram looked up and nodded his head. "You are right. Elden is a useless tree. He produces nothing for us to enjoy. What a waste of space in this big, empty, dry field."

The two travelers laughed and said mean things about the quiet tree. They forgot how hot they were just moments ago. They were very ungrateful for the wonderful shade that protected them right now.

Suddenly, a soft rustle came from the leaves. Elden the Plane Tree began to speak in a voice like whispering wind. He looked down at the two small, ungrateful men resting beneath his branches.

"You ungrateful people!" Elden said firmly. "You come here to escape the burning sun and enjoy my cool shade. Yet, you call me useless while you are still sitting safely within my strong arms."

Arlo and Bram jumped up in surprise. They looked at the giant tree and then at the hot, shimmering heat outside the shade. They realized how much they truly needed Elden right now.

The travelers felt very ashamed of their unkind words. They patted the smooth bark and thanked Elden for his kindness. Without his shade, they could not have finished their long and difficult journey.

We should always be thankful for the help we receive. Some gifts, like shade or kindness, cannot be eaten but are still very valuable. Never call a friend useless just because they are being quiet.
