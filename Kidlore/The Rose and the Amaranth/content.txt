Calantha and the Everlasting Bloom

In a sunny garden, Calantha the Rose bloomed with pride. Her petals were a deep, velvety red that sparkled under the morning dew. Everyone who passed by stopped to admire her stunning grace.

Calantha felt like the queen of the garden. She stretched her green leaves toward the warm sun and let her sweet perfume fill the air. She believed she was the most beautiful flower.

Near the vibrant Rose grew Amara the Amaranth. She was a sturdy plant with simple, purple blossoms. Amara did not have a sweet scent, but she stood tall and strong against the wind.

Calantha looked down at her neighbor with a tiny, elegant sigh. She wondered why any plant would choose to look so plain and humble when they could be as dazzling as a rose.

"Oh, Amara," Calantha whispered one sunny afternoon. "I truly pity you. You have no bright colors to show the world. You are just a simple herb while I am a glorious, famous flower."

Calantha tossed her head and preened her soft petals. "My beauty is celebrated by poets and artists everywhere. Everyone loves my shape and my fragrance. I am truly the luckiest plant alive today."

Amara listened quietly to the Rose’s boastful words. She did not feel sad or jealous. She simply smiled and continued to drink the cool water from the earth with her deep, strong roots.

"You are indeed lovely, Calantha," Amara replied kindly. "Your beauty is a gift that brings joy to the garden. I am happy to grow beside such a radiant and wonderful friend like you."

Soon, the hot summer days began to fade away. The air turned chilly, and the bright sun hid behind gray clouds. A cold wind started to blow through the once warm, peaceful garden.

Calantha’s bright red petals began to wrinkle and turn brown. Her sweet scent vanished into the autumn air. She felt weak and tired as the harsh winter weather drew closer to the garden.

One by one, Calantha’s beautiful petals fell onto the cold, damp ground. She looked at her bare stems and felt very sad. Her time of glory and fame had come to an end.

However, Amara the Amaranth remained exactly the same. Her purple flowers stayed fresh and vibrant despite the cold. She did not wither or lose her strength when the seasons changed for everyone.

Calantha looked at her friend in great surprise. "How can you still be so bright and healthy?" she asked. "I was beautiful, but now I am fading. You stay forever young and strong."

Amara replied, "Your beauty is like a flash of light, bright but brief. My worth is in my lasting life. I may be plain, but I bloom forever, even in the winter."

The moral of the story is that beauty is fleeting, but true character and inner strength endure. It is better to have lasting value than to rely on looks that quickly fade away.
