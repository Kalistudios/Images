Lilly and Leo's Rainbow Discovery

Gentle rain ended above the green park while bright sunshine suddenly appeared through the clouds. Lilly pointed excitedly toward a giant colorful rainbow stretching beautifully across the sky after the storm.

Lilly looked curiously at Leo and asked, “Why do rainbows appear in the sky after rain, and where do all those beautiful, glowing colors actually come from afterward during the afternoon?”

Leo smiled warmly and explained, “Rainbows appear when bright sunlight passes through tiny water droplets floating in the moist air after rain showers or near splashing waterfalls and garden fountains today.”

Lilly listened carefully before asking, “How can simple water droplets create colorful rainbows?” Leo nodded proudly and explained that clear water bends and separates white sunlight into many different beautiful colors quickly.”

Leo continued, “Bright sunlight may look white, but it actually contains many hidden colors including vibrant red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and deep violet shining inside the sky today.”

Lilly became curious and asked, “What happens when sunlight enters raindrops?” Leo smiled gently and explained that light bends while entering droplets, then reflects inside before leaving again toward the sky.”

Leo added, “Different colors bend slightly differently inside water droplets, causing the many colors to spread far apart and form giant, beautiful rainbows across the wide blue sky for everyone.”

Lilly looked thoughtful and asked, “Why does red stay above the rainbow?” Leo explained that each color bends differently, creating the same colorful rainbow order every single time we see them.”

Leo continued, “Rainbows usually appear when bright sunlight shines behind people while rain or tiny water droplets stay in front of them during typical daytime weather across the beautiful green park.”

Lilly asked curiously, “Can rainbows appear without rainstorms too?” Leo nodded wisely and explained that garden sprinklers, waterfalls, fountains, and ocean mist can also create colorful rainbows during the sunny day.”

Leo smiled and explained, “Sometimes people even see rare double rainbows when light reflects twice inside water droplets before leaving toward the sky afterward to create a very magical display today.”

Lilly became surprised and asked, “Can people ever reach the end of rainbows?” Leo nodded gently and explained that rainbows only appear from certain viewing angles instead of being solid objects.”

Leo continued, “As people move around, rainbows appear changing positions too because each person sees light from different water droplets in the air while they walk through the beautiful city park.”

Lilly happily understood that rainbows appear when sunlight bends and separates into many colors inside tiny water droplets floating through the air after rainfall during the warm and sunny afternoon today.”

As the glowing rainbow slowly faded from the evening sky, Leo gently reminded Lilly, “Nature creates beautiful surprises when sunlight, water, and curiosity come together perfectly after refreshing rainy days today.”
