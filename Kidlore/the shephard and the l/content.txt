The Shepherd and the Lion

Myron was a kind shepherd who looked after his sheep every single day. He walked through the green hills with a long wooden staff, watching the fluffy white clouds drift slowly across the sky.

One day, Myron heard a very loud and painful sound coming from the bushes. It was a golden lion named Aurum. Aurum was limping sadly and holding one of his large paws up.

Myron felt very scared at first because Aurum was a giant beast. However, he saw that the lion’s eyes were full of tears. The shepherd realized the poor lion was in great pain.

Myron walked slowly toward Aurum to see what was wrong. The lion stayed very still and held out his front paw. Myron saw a big, sharp thorn stuck deep inside the lion's skin.

"I will help you, friend," Myron whispered softly. He reached out with his steady hands and grabbed the sharp thorn. With one quick pull, the thorn came out and the lion felt better.

Aurum did not bite Myron. Instead, the lion rubbed his big head against the shepherd’s leg to say thank you. Then, the golden lion turned around and walked back into the deep forest.

Many months passed by, and the seasons changed. Myron found himself in a very dark and cold prison cell. He was very lonely and wondered if he would ever see his sheep again.

The next day, Myron was taken to a huge stone arena. The walls were very high and the ground was covered in sand. He knew he had to face a hungry lion.

A heavy iron gate opened with a loud creak. A hungry lion ran out into the bright sunlight, roaring loudly. Myron closed his eyes and waited for the big beast to attack him.

The lion stopped right in front of Myron. It was Aurum! The lion recognized the kind shepherd who had helped him before. Aurum did not growl or try to hurt his old friend.

Instead of attacking, Aurum sat down like a giant kitten. He gently licked Myron’s hands with his rough tongue. Myron opened his eyes and hugged the lion's thick, golden mane with joy.

The people watching from the seats were completely shocked. They had never seen a wild lion act so kindly toward a human. Myron and Aurum stood together in the middle of the arena.

Because of this amazing miracle, the leaders decided to let Myron go free. They also allowed Aurum to return to the wild forest. Both friends were very happy to be safe again.

Myron walked back to his green hills, and Aurum followed him for a little while. They had a bond that could never be broken. Kindness had saved both of their lives that day.

Moral: No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted. Those who show mercy to others will often find that mercy is returned to them when they need it the most.
