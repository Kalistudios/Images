Argos and the Water Dog

Argos the golden pup found a very large, juicy bone. He was so happy that he wagged his tail quickly. He thought it was the absolute best prize in the whole wide world.

Argos held the bone tightly in his mouth. He wanted to find a quiet spot to eat it all by himself. He felt like the luckiest dog in the entire sunny meadow.

He trotted along the path with his head held high. The sun was warm on his fur, and his bone felt heavy. Argos was very proud of his special and delicious treasure.

Soon, Argos came to a small wooden bridge over a clear stream. The water was very calm and looked like a bright mirror. He started to walk across the old wooden planks.

Halfway across, Argos stopped to look down. He saw a dog staring back at him from the water. That dog also had a bone, and it looked much bigger than his own.

Argos did not know he was looking at himself. He was very greedy and wanted the bigger bone too. He glared down at the water dog with a very grumpy, mean face.

The dog in the water glared back with the same mean look. Argos felt jealous and decided to take the other bone. He forgot how much he liked his very own prize.

Argos opened his mouth wide to bark a loud warning. He wanted to scare the other dog away. As he opened his jaw, his own bone slipped out and fell down, down.

Splash! The bone hit the water and sank to the bottom of the stream. Argos watched the ripples grow larger. Now, the dog in the water had no bone left either.

Argos realized that the other dog was just a reflection. He had been tricked by his own greed. He felt very silly standing on the bridge with his mouth feeling totally empty.

He looked down once more at the quiet water. The reflection was gone, and his bone was lost forever. Argos let out a tiny, sad whimper and his ears began to droop.

Argos walked home without any snack at all. He learned that being greedy can make you lose everything. From then on, he was always happy with the many treasures he already had.
