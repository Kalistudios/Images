Look Before You Leap

Once, two happy frogs named Theron and Ripple lived in a cozy pond. The water was cool and clear. They spent their days splashing around and catching many tasty, buzzing flies all day.

One summer, the sun became very hot and bright. The rain stopped falling from the blue sky. Their little pond began to shrink and turn into messy, brown mud. It was very sad.

"Our home is gone," Ripple said with a heavy sigh. The grass around them was dry and yellow. Theron nodded his head. They knew they had to find a new place to live.

The two friends began their long journey across the dusty fields. They hopped over rocks and under big leaves. They were very thirsty and hoped to find a big, splashing river very soon.

They hopped for a long time under the burning sun. Their skin felt very dry. Suddenly, Theron pointed his green toe toward a tall, stone circle standing in the middle of a field.

It was an old, deep well made of gray stones. The two frogs hopped quickly to the edge. They climbed up the mossy rocks and looked down into the dark, quiet hole below.

Far down at the very bottom, they saw a glimmer of silver water. It looked very cool and inviting. Theron let out a happy croak and did a little dance of joy.

"Look at that wonderful water!" Theron cried out. "It is deep and safe from the hot sun. We will never be thirsty again. Let us jump down into the well right now, friend!"

Theron moved to the very edge of the stone wall. He bent his strong legs, ready to leap. He could almost feel the cold water on his skin. He was very, very excited.

"Wait just one minute!" Ripple shouted, pulling Theron back from the ledge. Ripple looked down into the deep, dark well with a worried face. He started to think very hard about the plan.

Theron looked confused and tilted his head. "Why should we wait?" he asked. "The water is right there! It is perfect for us. We can swim and play in the deep, dark water."

Ripple pointed to the tall, steep walls of the well. "This well is very deep," he said softly. "The sides are smooth and high. If we go down, we cannot come back up."

"What if this water dries up too?" Ripple asked. "How would we get out of this deep hole? We would be stuck down there forever with no way to escape to the world."

Theron looked down and realized Ripple was right. The jump was easy, but the climb was impossible. They decided to keep walking until they found a safe pond with low, grassy banks instead.

The moral of this story is to look before you leap. Always think about the consequences of your actions before you act. It is wise to plan for what might happen next.
